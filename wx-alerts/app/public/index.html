Each NWS product is a row; products with tiers (radar indicated / observed / PDS / emergency, considerable / destructive) have one row per tier. The most specific matching tier decides. Announce = speak at home (MQTT) · Push = phone notification to each person whose position is inside the alert · Critical = max-importance channel that breaks through Do Not Disturb. Click a name to see and edit the wording that row will use: each level can have its own text or inherit from its product and then from the default wording.

SPC day 1 outlook sensors

The Storm Prediction Center's categorical outlook at home and at each person's position, as a number for automations: 0 none · 1 general thunderstorms · 2 marginal · 3 slight · 4 enhanced · 5 moderate · 6 high. One sensor per target (sensor.<prefix>_home, sensor.<prefix>_<person>) with the label, validity and forecaster as attributes; an event fires whenever a target's level changes. Created through MQTT discovery when MQTT is on, otherwise through the states API (then they disappear until the next poll after a Home Assistant restart).
