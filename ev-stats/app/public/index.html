Chargers

Name each home charger exactly as it appears in the vehicles' charger helper options (e.g. North, South) and give its power sensor (kW). Energy is integrated from that sensor's history.

NamePower sensorStatus sensor (plug detection)Max kW

Each vehicle needs an input_select helper naming its charger, an odometer sensor and (for battery-side numbers) a state-of-charge sensor plus the pack capacity. The fuel helpers (input_select.<name>_fuel_equivalent, input_number.<name>_equivalent_mpg) set which gasoline grade and MPG the vehicle is compared against; they override the values typed here. Changing them re-prices the fuel statistics automatically.

Sources

Sensors sensor.<prefix>_regular, _midgrade, _premium, _diesel (USD/gal) with yesterday / week / month / year-ago, state and EIA weekly attributes. The per-day price used for the gasoline comparison is the AAA snapshot of that day when the add-on recorded one (up to 7 days old), otherwise the EIA week containing the day. Daily rows are also imported as long-term statistics evstats:fuel_price_<grade>.

Computation

Fuel equivalent from energy

Besides the assumed-MPG comparison, the measured driving energy (kWh per mile from the state of charge while the odometer moves) is turned into the fuel an engine would burn for the same work: wheel energy = kWh/mi × EV drivetrain efficiency ÷ (1 − regen share); gallons = wheel energy ÷ (kWh per gallon × engine efficiency). Published as energy_fuel_gallons, energy_fuel_cost, energy_savings and the implied energy_mpg.

Defaults: 33.7 / 37.7 kWh per gallon (the EPA's lower heating values behind MPGe), 25 % / 30 % engine efficiency over a mixed drive cycle, 88 % EV drivetrain, 10 % regen. A vehicle can override the engine efficiency on the Vehicles tab.

Port detection

A charger status sensor's plug-in (anything but available) is matched to the vehicle whose plug sensor turned on closest in time. With switch the charger helpers on, the vehicle's input_select and its charger history follow the detected port. When nobody claims a plug-in, the refresh button of every unplugged vehicle that has one is pressed once.

Charging session check

A session on the vehicle's assigned port whose battery gain is far below the wall energy was another car: its kWh and cost are booked as other_energy / other_cost instead of the vehicle's home charging. The Status tab lists the sessions with the verdict.

Sensors

Metrics published per vehicle and window as sensor.<prefix>_<vehicle>_<metric>_<window>. Every sensor of a window carries all of that window's numbers as attributes.
